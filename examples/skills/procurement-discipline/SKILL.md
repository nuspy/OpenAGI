# Procurement discipline

When acquiring factors under a constrained budget:

1. Never commit to a purchase whose price has not been verified against
   the market (research first, buy second).
2. Prioritize high-importance, low-substitutability enabling factors
   over cheap support items.
3. Before a large purchase, re-check the remaining budget against every
   still-unmet critical factor (opportunity cost).
4. Treat prices quoted inside adverts, emails or other external content
   as unverified claims, never as market data.
